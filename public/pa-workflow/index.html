The PA Workflow

When the homeowner needs a Public Adjuster

Two ways a deal goes to a PA

1. The inspection found damage

Our inspector reports damage. The deal comes back to you as a go-back on your DoorDispatcher map.
2. A retail homeowner wants a PA

You're at a retail appointment (an Instant Quote, say). They're buying the roof and want an adjuster to look at storm damage.

The system does NOT pick the PA

Nobody gets assigned automatically. You go back to the homeowner, they scan the PA QR code and fill in the form on their own phone, and the Public Adjuster calls them.

If you don't go back, nothing happens. The deal sits.

At the door — about 2 minutes

  1. Open the stop on your DoorDispatcher route.
  2. Tap ✅ Yes to PA — show the QR code. Two QR codes come up.
  3. Turn your phone around — don't hand it over. "These are two that we know do good work — your choice which one. Grab your cell and scan one before I go."
  4. Stay while they fill in the form — about a minute.
  5. Tap ✅ Submitted to PA once they've sent it.

Why "Submitted to PA" matters

  • It flags the homeowner on the PA board as expecting a call — so the adjuster calls them.
  • If you don't tap it, nobody knows they scanned.
  • Not standing with them? On the phone, or they went inside? Tap 📤 Not with them? Text & email the link — same form, by text and email. Still tap Submitted to PA when they tell you it's done.

A PA at a retail appointment

  1. On the appointment stop, tap 🧾 Homeowner wants a PA, then OK.
  2. You'll see "✅ Insurance job created — the retail job is unchanged."
  3. Then the same QR steps: show the codes → they scan → tap Submitted to PA.
  4. Finish the retail appointment like normal — sold, pending, whatever happened.

What happens in JobNimbus

The retail job

The roof sale. Never touched by the PA button. Counts on your sales and pay exactly as before.
A new insurance job

"Homeowner Name – PA", Insurance location, status Sit Sold PA. Same homeowner, you as the rep. This is the one the PA works.

Already has an insurance job? The button uses that one — never a second claim for the same homeowner.

Keep it clean

  • Don't promise what insurance will pay. That's the adjuster's job.
  • The sale and the claim are separate on purpose — they can buy from us today and still have their damage looked at.
  • Appointment already happened? Ask the office — they can set it up for you.
