Virtual Training

The new flow · start to finish

Everything a trainee sees, and everything the system now does on its own.

The big change

Our own meeting rooms. No more Zoom.

  • Every trainee gets their own link: no app, no meeting ID, their real name on their tile.
  • Attendance takes itself: who joined, when, how long, camera off.
  • Reps see their rooms on their personal dashboard (LIVE NOW / Next: time).
  • Rooms for each team, the company meeting, the 9:15 Devotional, Week A and Week B.

Step 2 · Sign in

First name, last name, email. It finds them on this week's class list. No kiosk.

Step 3 · Paperwork first

W-9 + contractor agreement open right there, then "✅ Done, go to training" brings them back. Nobody gets into class until it's signed.

Step 3 · Safety net

Left: if their email doesn't match, the paperwork goes by text + email instead ("check your junk mail"). Right: banking can wait on Day 1, but from the next day it's required before class.

Step 4 · The lobby

Done early? A slideshow sells them on the company: 4.8★ from 301 Google reviews, real 5-star reviews, past trainees in their own words, and the "before training starts" checklist. It lets them in when the doors open.

Step 5 · In class: the trainer's tools
  • 📊 Present: Week A Day 1 on everyone's screen, sharp, trainer as a circle. Arrows move everyone together. No Keynote.
  • ⏺ Record: mutes everyone else, everyone to speaker view, recording goes to the room's recordings page.
  • 🎙 Podcast mode: put a guest and yourself "on stage"; everyone sees only you two.
  • Host controls: mute / unmute one or everyone, remove someone, see cameras.
  • 🎭 Practice: the AI homeowner joins as a tile and talks with the trainee you pick.
Be on time

The doors close 2 minutes in

2 minutes after the start time (or after the trainer arrives, if the trainer is late). Week A Day 1: "call Brent to reschedule". Any other day: the being-on-time message. Anyone already in can always rejoin.

Miss a day

Missed the last class day = the link stops working. "Force into Week B" is the override.

Step 6 · Field days

Day 2: into the field

  • Signing in on Day 2 makes them a field trainee, so their regional manager sees them.
  • William's picker shows each trainee's name, home city and phone from Day 2. He plans rides around Florida, not the training center.
  • Thu, Fri, Sat: they knock doors on DoorDispatcher.
Step 7 · Show the effort

Week B needs an average of 30 doors a day over Week A Thu–Sat. Days with William don't count against them.

The second chance

Click it. Then prove it.

  • They click 🔥 I still want Week B and I'll prove it, and we see Committed (with the time). No click = they're gone.
  • Average 30 doors a day, Monday–Friday.
  • Saturday morning the system enrolls them in next week's Week B and texts + emails their link.
  • Their manager is still there to help all week.
We can fix results. We can't fix effort.
