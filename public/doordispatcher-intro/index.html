DoorDispatcher

How to use it — your field partner

What it does for you

  • 🗺️ Shows you which doors to knock — no guessing, no wasted driving.
  • 🧭 Builds your route for the day in the tightest order.
  • 🖊️ Signs the homeowner right there — it lands in our system credited to you.
  • ⏳ Remembers every come-back so no deal falls through the cracks.
  • 🤝 It's your accountability partner: your effort is visible, so your hard work gets noticed.
🗺️

Reading your map & pins

Every time you open the map
Each dot is a door; its color is the type. The legend on the right lists every type with a count. Gold stars are roofs we already installed — reference only. Tap a pin to see the homeowner, what's happened there, and which lead type it is.

🚪 Your leads

🖊️ Inspection Lead
A door that needs a free roof inspection. Knock and get the homeowner signed up for one.
↩️ IQ – Not Interested
Homeowners who got an Instant Quote online but said no. Go win them back in person.

Your map is set to these two. Each pin's card tells you exactly which one it is.

Getting your route

▶ Start my day
Builds one tight loop. Any come-backs due today become required stops, and fresh doors fill in around them.
▢ Route an area
Drag a box around the doors you want to work right now — it routes exactly those, in order.
📅 Plan your day
Have an appointment? Enter your start time — it drops the appointment in at its time and fills the gaps with doors so you're never late.
🧭 Manager planned it
See the purple banner? Your manager carved out your section. Just tap Start my day.
🧭

Your route card

Pops up at every stop: DOOR X of Y, ✏️ Edit route, 📍 Re-route from where I am, the homeowner name + address, and 🧭 Directions (Google or Apple Maps). Then you status the door.

✅ How'd it go? — status every door

🖊️ Sign Inspection — yes to a free roof inspection.
❌ Not Interested — logs it, the door is done.
⏳ Pending — come back another time; set the day and time.
🏠 BTR appt — they'd rather go retail; book it right there.
🏠 Not home — logs the knock; the door stays workable.
🆕 New Roof — already has a new roof.

You status a door once you're actually there — the GPS confirms it.

🖊️

Sign an inspection

The homeowner says yes
Tap Sign Inspection. The form opens pre-filled with their address — confirm the details and submit. The door turns Sold, it's in the system credited to you. No paperwork tonight.

More ways to win

🍀 Clover Leaf
Green pins around a roof our crew is installing. The easiest doors on the map — pitch the neighbor by name.
⭐ Referral
A homeowner sends you to a friend? Enter them — a gold pin drops and they land in your calls to make.
📍 Drop your own pin
Spot a damaged roof not on the map? Add the house — you get the credit as self-generated.
🔁 Rounds — 3 a day
Finish a route and it rebuilds your not-homes and come-backs into a new, shorter one. Top earners run all three.
⏳

Go-backs & come-backs

After you sign someone up, ask their typical availability (e.g. Tues & Wed at 5). That's a tentative come-back to go over their inspection results. The map routes you to be in that area around then — it never forgets.

📲 What happens next

  1. You get a text and an email with your DoorDispatcher link — right now.
  2. Open it and watch the How to Use DoorDispatcher video.
  3. Take the test — it unlocks your map.
  4. Save the link to your home screen. That's your map in the field.
