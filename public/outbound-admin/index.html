Outbound Dispatch

Your admin page — running the call center

Getting in

  • You have your own link to this page — bookmark it. Best on a computer.
  • The first time, you make a PIN (4–6 numbers). It asks every time after that.
  • Forgot it? The office can reset it for you.
  • At the top: 📞 Call Someone (a quick video call) and 📅 Schedule Interviews — and a banner when you have a meeting today.

🎧 Your callers

  1. + Add a caller — name, cell, email.
  2. 📍 Calling today — tap the county each caller works today.
  3. 📲 Send them their link — they get their calling page by text and email (or 📋 Copy link).
  4. ✏️ Edit to change details or turn a caller off.

📝 The call script

Paste the script here and Save script. It shows beside every call on the callers' page.

The blanks fill themselves: the first ____ becomes the caller's first name, the next one the homeowner's mobile number.

📅 Wants an appointment instead

Some homeowners don't want the free inspection — they want a rep to come out and give a price. Your callers can now book that.

  • They tap 📅 Wants an appointment instead, confirm the address, and pick from the open times.
  • Every one is credited to your team.
  • Two choices, always: "Tomorrow or Wednesday?" → "Morning or evening?" → "A 5 or a 7?" Never "when works for you?"
  • Never same-day — tomorrow or the day after, unless they name a day. Both decision-makers must be there.

📅 Book an appointment

  1. Tap 📅 Book an appointment on your page.
  2. Confirm the address — the open times show up.
  3. Pick one and book it. Same rules as your callers. It counts for your team.

You get a text every time an appointment is booked for your team.

📲 Callbacks go to Zilla

  • When a homeowner calls back, Zilla answers — our AI receptionist.
  • If a caller already called them, she knows who they are.
  • Free inspection? She texts the Yes link and waits on the line while they tap it.
  • Wants an appointment? She books it for your team.
  • Your callers never take incoming calls or chase callbacks.

🤖 From Zilla

✅ Booked — appointments she booked for your team
📞 Couldn't book — call them — people she couldn't set up (for example, no rep covers that area)

It's in the 📅 Rep appointments card near the top of your page. Follow up on every "Couldn't book."
