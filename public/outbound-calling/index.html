The Calling Page

Booking free roof inspections by phone

Before you start

  • Open your own calling link on a computer, in Chrome. Bookmark it.
  • Make your PIN the first time (4–6 numbers).
  • Headset on. When the browser asks to use your microphone, click Allow.
  • The top shows the county you're calling today and your count: calls and Yes links.

One homeowner at a time

The page shows the homeowner's name, address and number — and if they were called before, what happened.

Tap 📞 Call. The homeowner sees our company number. During the call: Mute, Hang up, and a timer.

The script sits beside it with your name and their number already filled in.

When they say yes

  1. Tap 👍 They said YES — text them the link.
  2. Stay on the line. Ask them to open the text and tap I approve.
  3. The page tells you live: ⏳ Waiting for them to open it → 👀 They opened it.
  4. 🎉 They tapped I approve! — the free inspection is booked. Thank them → Next home →

What the homeowner sees

  • The text opens this page on their phone — their name and address at the top.
  • A sample of the free roof certificate they'll get.
  • One short agreement, then the big green I approve button.
  • Below it: our company info and Florida license number — what the script promised.

📅 Wants an appointment instead

Some homeowners don't want the free inspection — they just want a rep to come out and give them a price.

  1. Tap 📅 Wants an appointment instead.
  2. Confirm the address with them.
  3. The page shows the open times.
  4. Book it right there. It's credited to Jeff Mann's team.

Two choices, every time

"I have tomorrow or Wednesday — which is better?"

"Morning or evening?"

"I have a 5 and a 7 — which is better?"

Always give two choices. Never ask "when works for you?" The page writes each line for you.

Appointment rules

  • Never same-day. Offer tomorrow or the day after.
  • Only go further out if they name a day.
  • Both decision-makers must be there — ask: "Will your spouse be home too?"

Every other answer

🕒 They'll do it later — the link is theirs to tap when ready
⏰ Call back later — pick the time; it comes back to you
📵 No answer · 🙅 Not interested
🏠 Has a roofer / new roof · 👤 Not the owner
❌ Wrong / bad number
🚫 Do NOT call again — they're never called again. Use it whenever they ask.

📲 Callbacks go to Zilla

  • When a homeowner calls our number back, Zilla answers — our AI receptionist.
  • If you already called them, she knows who they are.
  • Free inspection? She texts the Yes link and waits on the line while they tap it — just like you.
  • Wants an appointment? She books it for Jeff Mann's team.
  • You never take incoming calls or chase callbacks. Just keep dialing.

🎓 Practice calls

  • New? Jeff gives you practice calls first — the purple banner shows how many are left.
  • Tap 📞 Call — practice homeowner. You talk to an AI homeowner on the computer — allow the microphone.
  • Do everything for real: script, Yes link, appointment. Then tap what happened.
  • Nothing real is booked, practice calls never count toward pay, and Jeff can listen.

The rules

  • Calling hours: 9 AM–8 PM Eastern, Monday–Saturday. Outside them the page shows "no calls right now."
  • If they ask not to be called — 🚫 Do NOT call again, every time.
  • Calls may be recorded — say the line on screen at the start.
  • Only text the link to someone who said yes on the call.
  • 🧪 TEST homes are practice calls — nothing real gets booked.

What counts

You're paid on homeowners who tap I approve.

So the call isn't done when they say yes — it's done when the page says 🎉 They tapped I approve! Stay on the line until it does.
